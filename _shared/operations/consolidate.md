# Operation: consolidate

A periodic deep pass that makes the wiki deeper, the way sleep consolidates memory. Use this when the human asks to consolidate, synthesize, or look for patterns across the wiki, in any wording. It isn't lint: lint keeps the wiki healthy.

**Follow:** the shared rules (the top folder's `AGENTS.md`) and this wiki's `AGENTS.md`, which says whether consolidation output is written directly or proposed for ratification.

**Focus:** the area the human named, or the whole wiki.

## Steps

1. **Re-read** `index.md` and the page graph, scoped to the focus if there is one.
2. **Surface patterns** that have emerged across many sources: recurring themes, clusters, and, in a preserve-tension wiki, the productive tensions worth making explicit.
3. **Propose pages** where a theme recurs without a page of its own.
4. **Re-link:** strengthen the links and typed edges that one-at-a-time ingests missed.
5. **Write the report** to `_state/consolidation-YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

**Authorship:** in a wiki with an authorship gate, everything here is a proposal for the human to ratify, and you never author positions. In a reconcile wiki, additive re-linking may apply directly, but new synthesis that changes the message goes to review.

**Finish:** log `## [YYYY-MM-DD] consolidate | <scope>`, commit with a message like `<wiki-folder>: consolidate <scope>`, then walk the human through the proposals.
