# Call summary: Copperline Cafes, 2026-03-12

Our CEO spoke with Copperline's operations manager for 30 minutes.

## What's working

They like the taste and consistency of our house blend. Their customers notice when they switch roasters, so they don't switch lightly.

## Delivery

The two late deliveries in February caused real trouble: one cafe ran out of coffee on a Saturday morning. Reliability matters more to them than price.

## Ironvale's pitch

Ironvale approached them last month, offering free delivery and its new subscription discount. They haven't decided anything, and they asked whether we'd match free delivery.

## Renewal

They'll decide on the renewal by mid-June, before the contract ends on June 30. They didn't say how much coffee they'd buy next year.

## What they'd value

A freshness guarantee interests them, and they agreed to join the April pilot. They also asked for a text alert on delivery days.
