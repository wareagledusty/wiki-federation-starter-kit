# Copperline Cafes

**What it is:** a chain of nine cafes, and Kestrel Bay's largest wholesale account. (Fictional example.)

## Summary

Copperline buys about 400 pounds of coffee a month, and its contract renews on June 30, 2026. [src: 2026-03-02-leadership-meeting.md#copperline-cafes] It will decide on the renewal by mid-June. [src: 2026-03-12-copperline-call.md#renewal] Reliability matters more to Copperline than price, and February's two late deliveries caused real trouble. [src: 2026-03-12-copperline-call.md#delivery] [Ironvale Coffee](ironvale-coffee.md) is pitching it free delivery and a subscription discount. [src: 2026-03-12-copperline-call.md#ironvales-pitch]

## Key facts

* Nine cafes, buying about 400 pounds a month. [src: 2026-03-02-leadership-meeting.md#copperline-cafes]
* It likes the taste and consistency of Kestrel Bay's house blend, and doesn't switch roasters lightly. [src: 2026-03-12-copperline-call.md#whats-working]
* It asked whether Kestrel Bay would match free delivery. [src: 2026-03-12-copperline-call.md#ironvales-pitch]
* It agreed to join the [freshness guarantee](freshness-guarantee.md) pilot, and asked for a text alert on delivery days. [src: 2026-03-12-copperline-call.md#what-theyd-value]
