# Freshness guarantee

**In one line:** a promise that coffee arrives within 72 hours of roasting, or the order is free.

## What it means

Kestrel Bay's leadership approved a pilot of this guarantee on 2026-03-02, with two accounts from April 1 to May 31, after which it decides whether to offer it to every account. [src: 2026-03-02-leadership-meeting.md#decisions] Keeping the promise depends on an on-time roasting schedule, which slipped twice in February. *(Synthesis, from [src: 2026-03-02-leadership-meeting.md#copperline-cafes].)*

## Where it shows up

* [Freshness guarantee pilot](../initiatives/freshness-guarantee-pilot.md): the initiative that tests it.
* [Copperline Cafes](copperline-cafes.md): agreed to join the pilot. [src: 2026-03-12-copperline-call.md#what-theyd-value]
* [Ironvale Coffee](ironvale-coffee.md): its price sheet makes no promise about time since roasting. [src: 2026-03-09-ironvale-price-sheet.md#delivery]

## Settled view

Among the roasters this wiki tracks, only Kestrel Bay offers this guarantee. [src: 2026-03-09-ironvale-price-sheet.md#what-it-means-for-us]
