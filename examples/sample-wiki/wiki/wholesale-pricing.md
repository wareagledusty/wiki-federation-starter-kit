# Wholesale pricing

**In one line:** what cafes and offices pay per pound for coffee bought in bulk, and the delivery terms that come with it.

## What it means

In this market, roasters quote a per-pound price for a house blend, often with an order minimum, and delivery terms change what a buyer actually pays. *(Synthesis, from the price points below.)*
