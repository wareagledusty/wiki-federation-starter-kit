# Ironvale Coffee

**What it is:** a competing wholesale roaster that sells to cafes and offices. (Fictional example.)

## Summary

Ironvale's house blend costs $10.75 a pound on orders of 50 pounds or more, as of March 1, 2026. [src: 2026-03-09-ironvale-price-sheet.md#pricing] It delivers free on orders over 100 pounds [src: 2026-03-09-ironvale-price-sheet.md#delivery], and it launched a subscription program at a 5 percent discount, with a six-month minimum. [src: 2026-03-09-ironvale-price-sheet.md#programs] It has pitched both to [Copperline Cafes](copperline-cafes.md). [src: 2026-03-12-copperline-call.md#ironvales-pitch]

## Key facts

* House blend: $10.75 a pound on orders of 50 pounds or more, effective March 1, 2026. [src: 2026-03-09-ironvale-price-sheet.md#pricing]
* Single-origin coffees: $13.50 to $16.00 a pound. [src: 2026-03-09-ironvale-price-sheet.md#pricing]
* It delivers weekly, with no promise of how soon after roasting the coffee arrives. [src: 2026-03-09-ironvale-price-sheet.md#delivery]
