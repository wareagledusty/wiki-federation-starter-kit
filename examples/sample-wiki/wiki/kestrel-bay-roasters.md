# Kestrel Bay Roasters

**What it is:** a wholesale coffee roaster that sells to cafes and offices. This wiki belongs to Kestrel Bay. (Fictional example.)

## Summary

Kestrel Bay ended February 2026 with 48 active wholesale accounts, down from 51, and volume flat at about 2,900 pounds a month. [src: 2026-03-02-leadership-meeting.md#wholesale-accounts] It sells its house blend at $10.40 a pound. [src: 2026-03-02-leadership-meeting.md#competitors] Its largest account is [Copperline Cafes](copperline-cafes.md). [src: 2026-03-02-leadership-meeting.md#copperline-cafes]

## Key facts

* It lost three small office accounts to a cheaper supplier this quarter. [src: 2026-03-02-leadership-meeting.md#wholesale-accounts]
* It made two late deliveries to Copperline in February, caused by its roasting schedule slipping. [src: 2026-03-02-leadership-meeting.md#copperline-cafes]
* It's piloting a [freshness guarantee](freshness-guarantee.md) from April 1 to May 31. [src: 2026-03-02-leadership-meeting.md#decisions]

## Related

Kestrel Bay competes with [Ironvale Coffee](ironvale-coffee.md) on [wholesale pricing](wholesale-pricing.md) and delivery terms.
